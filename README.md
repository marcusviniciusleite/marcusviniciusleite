## Teaching

I teach undergraduate courses in **Computer Science**, **Information Systems**, and related fields, covering topics such as programming, information systems, data analysis, artificial intelligence, and emerging computing technologies.

## Current Research

My current research explores the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence, Paraconsistent Logic, Data Analytics, and Decision Support Systems**, with particular interest in methods for dealing with uncertainty, inconsistency, and contradictory information.

I am also interested in **Quantum Computing**, particularly its computational foundations, noisy quantum systems, and methods for evaluating the reliability of quantum circuits.
